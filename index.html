How Each Algorithm Decides & Bélády's Anomaly

Core intuition for FIFO, LRU, and Optimal replacement, plus live verification of FIFO's anomaly.

FIFO (First-In, First-Out)

Evicts Oldest Load

Keeps frames in a simple arrival queue and evicts whichever page was loaded earliest, even if that page was accessed again recently.

Decision Clue
Earliest load step
Bélády Anomaly
Vulnerable

LRU (Least Recently Used)

Evicts Stale Access

Tracks when each resident page was last used and replaces whichever page has gone untouched longest, preserving active working-set pages.

Decision Clue
Earliest last access
Bélády Anomaly
Immune (Stack property)

Optimal (Bélády's MIN)

Evicts Farthest Future

Scans future references to replace the page needed farthest in the future (or never reused). Establishes the theoretical minimum faults.

Decision Clue
Farthest next use
Bélády Anomaly
Immune (Optimal bound)
